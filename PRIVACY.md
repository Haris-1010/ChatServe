# ChatServe Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

ChatServe is a WhatsApp assistant that restaurants use to answer customer questions, take orders and table bookings, and pass conversations to their staff. This policy explains what information ChatServe handles when you message a restaurant that uses it.

## Information we collect
When you message a restaurant's WhatsApp number that runs ChatServe, we receive and store:
- your WhatsApp phone number and WhatsApp profile name;
- the messages you send and the replies sent to you;
- order and booking details you provide, such as your name, delivery address, area, items, payment method and booking time;
- basic technical data about each message (message ID, time, delivery status).

We do not collect your contacts, location (unless you type an address), photos or other data from your phone.

## How we use it
We use this information only to:
- reply to your questions about the restaurant, its menu, prices, delivery and timings;
- create, confirm and track your orders and table bookings;
- send your order or booking to the restaurant's staff;
- let restaurant staff read and reply to your chat;
- keep a short chat history so replies stay relevant.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

## Who we share it with
- **The restaurant you message**, so it can prepare your order or booking and reply to you.
- **Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform)**, which delivers the messages.
- **Anthropic**, an AI provider that generates replies. It receives the restaurant's menu and the recent messages of your chat to produce a reply.
- **Hosting and database providers** that store the data on our behalf.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

## How long we keep it
Chats, orders and bookings are kept while the restaurant uses ChatServe, so it can see past orders and support you. You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time (see below).

## Your choices and data deletion
You can stop messaging at any time. To ask for a copy or deletion of your data, contact us at the email below with the phone number you used. We will delete your chats, orders and bookings within 30 days, unless we must keep something by law.

## Security
Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access to the restaurant dashboard is password protected.

## Children
ChatServe is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes
We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
